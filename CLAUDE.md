# Project: Crada
Prediction market intelligence platform. Live at crada.fun (Netlify). Local: ~/foresight.
Hackathon deadline: 2026-05-11 — Colosseum Frontier (Solana).

## Stack
Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Privy auth, Solana wallet adapter

## Brand
- Tagline: "Prediction markets, made easy"
- Voice: mysterious, confident, insider knowledge
- Colors: black background, purple (#7c3aed) accent

## What's built
- 500+ live Polymarket markets with images, trending, category filters, search, show more
- Signals tab with insider/whale/volume detection
- Market detail pages with risk scenarios, Crada signal, trade buttons (Polymarket + Kalshi)
- Privy auth — email + Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare)
- Responsive grid — 5 columns desktop, 2 mobile
- Footer with socials and legal
